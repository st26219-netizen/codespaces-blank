Hinduism

Hinduism is one of the oldest religions in the world. It started in India thousands of years ago. Many people follow Hinduism and practice different traditions and beliefs.

Important Things in Hinduism

  • Belief in many gods and goddesses
  • Karma – good and bad actions affect life
  • Dharma – doing the right thing
  • Reincarnation – being reborn after death
  • Yoga and meditation
